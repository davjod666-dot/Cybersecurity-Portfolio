## 2. Data and information flows

Document input sources, data sensitivity, output recipients, retention, logging, access controls, and third-party processing. Identify what can be redacted, minimised, or excluded. Record unresolved data-handling questions.

Define the likelihood and impact scales for the actual use case before assigning ratings. Do not use numerical scores without stating their meaning and supporting assumptions.

## 4. Control validation

For each control, record the objective, implementation, test method, expected outcome, observed outcome, evidence reference, and limitations. Include representative failure cases and escalation behaviour.

## 5. Human oversight

Record which decisions require human approval, who may override or stop the workflow, how a reviewer checks the evidence, and how an incident is escalated. Test the stop and recovery procedures.

## 6. Decision and residual risk

Record open findings, control gaps, risk acceptance authority, deployment conditions, and follow-up dates. Separate technical validation from legal, privacy, or regulatory review.

## 7. Change and monitoring

Identify changes that trigger reassessment: model versions, prompts, datasets, integrations, tool permissions, intended users, or operating context. Specify monitoring ownership and review cadence.

## References and scope

This practical template is informed by the lifecycle approach of the [NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) and [Microsoft's responsible AI resources](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/ai/responsible-ai). It is not a complete framework crosswalk, legal checklist, or certification assessment.
